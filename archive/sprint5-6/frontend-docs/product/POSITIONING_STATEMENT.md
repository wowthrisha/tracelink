## The Core Problem SecureDoc Solves

When a professional shares a document via email or Google Drive, they lose all control over what happens next. The document can be downloaded, forwarded, printed, and distributed to anyone. The sender gets no signal that it was ever read. And once shared, it cannot be taken back.

This is not a hypothetical problem. For consultants, architects, engineers, and founders, it happens regularly — a proposal forwarded to a competitor, a calculation package extracted for reuse, an investor deck circulated outside the intended conversation, a pricing sheet leaked into a competitive evaluation. The professional world has been living with this problem and accepting it as the cost of doing business.

SecureDoc solves it.

---

## Formal Positioning Statement

**For** professional services firms and solo operators — consultants, architects, engineers, and founders — who share proprietary documents with external parties as a core business activity,

**SecureDoc is a** secure document sharing platform,

**that** gives document owners complete control over who sees their work, how long they see it, and what they can do with it — while showing exactly which pages captured attention, what questions viewers asked, and how the conversation should continue.

**Unlike** email attachments, Google Drive, and DocSend — which treat sharing as a one-way delivery action — **SecureDoc** treats every shared document as an ongoing, revocable conversation with full visibility and enforcement.

## Positioning by Segment

### For Consultants and Advisory Firms
**Headline:** *Your report stays yours.*

You spend weeks on a deliverable. Once you email the PDF, you have no idea if it was read, forwarded to a competitor, or sitting unread in an inbox. SecureDoc gives you a link that tracks every page view, lets you revoke access after the engagement ends, and surfaces client questions directly from the document. No more "did you get a chance to read it?"

### For Architecture and Engineering Firms
**Headline:** *Issue once. Update everywhere. Know who read it.*

Drawing sets and specifications are your intellectual property. When you issue them to contractors and clients, you should know who opened them, which pages they spent time on, and whether they're working from the current version. SecureDoc's revocable links, domain restrictions, and per-page analytics turn document delivery into an auditable professional process.

### For Startup Founders
**Headline:** *Know exactly which slide stopped every investor.*

You're in a fundraising process. Your deck is live. You need to know which investors actually engaged — and which page made them stop. SecureDoc shows you per-slide dwell time for every viewer, lets you password-protect and expire links for each stage of your process, and lets investors leave questions directly on the slides they care about. DocSend shows you that someone opened your deck. SecureDoc shows you what they thought about it.

### For Sales Professionals
**Headline:** *Know when to call before you call.*

Stop guessing whether your prospect read the proposal. SecureDoc tells you when they opened it, how long they spent on the pricing page, and — when they leave a question on the document — exactly what's on their mind. Send the follow-up at exactly the right moment.

SecureDoc occupies the high-control + high-analytics quadrant for the SMB market — a position no current tool holds well.

- **Google Drive / Dropbox**: No DRM, no expiry, no analytics. Free tier "good enough" competition.
- **DocSend**: Strong analytics, weak access control (no IP allowlist, no concurrent session limits, no viewer annotations). Expensive at scale. Sales-focused positioning misses professional services.
- **Digify**: Strong DRM, weak analytics, no feedback loop, dated UX.
- **Egnyte / SharePoint**: Strong governance, but enterprise-only pricing, IT-required, no SMB path.

## What SecureDoc Is Not

SecureDoc is not a project management tool. It doesn't replace Procore, Autodesk, or Asana.

SecureDoc is not an e-signature platform. It doesn't replace DocuSign or PandaDoc.

SecureDoc is not a file storage service. Documents are shared, not stored for internal access.

SecureDoc is a document sharing and intelligence layer — what happens between "I sent it" and "they read it and we need to talk."

## Brand Personality

**What SecureDoc should feel like:** A precise, professional tool. Not consumer. Not enterprise bureaucracy. The aesthetic equivalent of a well-made instrument — dark, confident, technically capable, fast.

**Not:** Playful, casual, startup-meme-culture. Not corporate-heavy with stock photos of handshakes.

**Tone in copy:** Direct. No marketing fluff. Say what the product does. "Track every page view" not "unlock powerful document intelligence." "Revoke a link in one click" not "seamlessly manage access across your organization."
